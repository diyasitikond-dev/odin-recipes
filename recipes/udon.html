Udon Recipe

Description

This udon recipe is a comforting and filling dish that showcases the versatility of udon noodles. Perfect for a warm meal on a cold day, this recipe is easy to follow and can be customized with your favorite vegetables and proteins.

Ingredients

  • 200g udon noodles
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon mirin
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger
  • 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

Steps

  1. Cook the udon noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat the soy sauce over medium heat. Add the mirin and sugar. Stir well and let it simmer for 5-10 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
  3. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger to the skillet. Stir well and let it cook for another 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add the cooked noodles to the skillet and toss it with the sauce until well coated.
  5. Grated Parmesan cheese for serving
