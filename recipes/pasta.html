Pasta Recipe

Description

This pasta recipe is a classic Italian dish that combines fresh ingredients to create a flavorful and satisfying meal. Perfect for a family dinner or a special occasion, this recipe is easy to follow.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

Steps

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the diced tomatoes and dried basil to the skillet. Stir well and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
  4. Season the sauce with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Add the cooked spaghetti to the skillet and toss it with the sauce until well coated.
  6. Serve the pasta hot, garnished with grated Parmesan cheese.
