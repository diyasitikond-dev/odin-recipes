Peanut Noodles Recipe

Description

This peanut noodles recipe is a delicious and easy-to-make dish that combines the rich flavor of peanut butter with the freshness of vegetables. Perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or a light lunch, this recipe is sure to satisfy your taste buds.

Ingredients

  • 200g ramen noodles
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon grated fresh ginger

Steps

  1. Cook the ramen noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat the peanut butter over medium heat. Add the soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sugar. Stir well and let it simmer for 5-10 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
  3. Add the minced garlic and grated ginger to the skillet. Stir well and let it cook for another 2-3 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add the cooked noodles to the skillet and toss it with the sauce until well coated.
  5. Grated Parmesan cheese for serving
